\paragraph{Task 5: static-target occlusion and recovery.}
The sequence contained seven occlusions with a median duration of
3.20 s. The platform reference did not move
during any occlusion, so this task characterizes update suppression, pose
holding, and reacquisition rather than prediction through hidden object motion.
EgoAnchor maintained 100\% display availability and a median event-level
occlusion translation P95 of
1.82 mm,
compared with
13.00 mm
for One-Euro Anchor. No harmful output jump above 10 mm or
5$^\circ$ occurred for EgoAnchor during the seven occlusions, whereas
One-Euro Anchor produced 14
such jumps.

\paragraph{VCD admission attribution.}
Disabling VCD increased the median event-level occlusion translation P95 from
1.82 to 23.97 mm
(92\% reduction) and introduced
10 harmful output jumps, compared
with zero for EgoAnchor. The benefit was concentrated in four events; the
remaining three events were equivalent within a 0.001-mm numerical tolerance because no
low-score harmful candidate crossed the admission boundary.

At the observed score threshold of 0.15, VCD
retained 98.3\% of pose candidates. Nine of the ten
rejected candidates exceeded the frozen harmful-candidate criterion
(translation error $>$20 mm or rotation error $>$10$^\circ$), corresponding to
90\% rejection precision. However, this
removed only 43\% of all harmful candidates:
twelve high-score harmful candidates remained accepted. Consistently, the
P95-translation risk--coverage AURC was
4.59 mm, better than a random ordering
(8.89 mm) but above the error oracle
(2.80 mm).

\paragraph{Recovery and failure boundary.}
Fresh post-visible output appeared after a median of
208 ms, and durable
recovery required a median of
208 ms. Six events
recovered promptly. In Event 7, a sequence of high-score but increasingly
incorrect candidates passed the VCD gate, causing the translation error to
exceed 60 mm and delaying durable recovery to
6436 ms. This failure
demonstrates that VCD is a useful runtime ranking signal, not a calibrated
correctness probability or a guarantee against high-confidence drift.
