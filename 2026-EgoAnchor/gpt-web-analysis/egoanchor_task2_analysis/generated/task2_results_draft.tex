\paragraph{Task 2: start--stop 6DoF motion.}
The sequence contained five marker-defined start--stop cycles over 89.9 s.
Statistics were first computed within each cycle and then summarized across cycles; render frames were used only to form continuous trajectories.
EgoAnchor produced a median visible response of 751 ms and met the predefined stable-output criterion in 600 ms.
Its median event-level motion translation error was 30.1 mm, with a median event P95 of 75.4 mm.

\paragraph{Temporal-synthesis attribution.}
Removing temporal synthesis reduced median motion translation error by 2.7 mm and removed approximately 66 ms of output-target lag, but increased the median event P99 per-frame translation jump from 6.7 to 20.9 mm.
Thus, temporal synthesis reduced large discrete jumps by 68\%, establishing a continuity--accuracy trade-off rather than an unconditional error improvement.

\paragraph{StaticLock attribution.}
Disabling StaticLock shortened the median visible response from 751 to 291 ms, but increased median post-stop settling from 600 to 1170 ms.
The complete system re-entered its logged static-lock state after a median of 667 ms.
